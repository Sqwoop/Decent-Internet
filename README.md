# Decent-Internet

**A better internet starts with the people who use it.**

Decent-Internet is an open-source initiative to develop affordable, decentralized, privacy-first internet infrastructure.

Our vision is simple: **the internet should serve its users, not exploit them.**

---

## Our Vision

We believe the internet should be a shared resource that benefits the people who use it, contribute to it, and help maintain it.

Decent-Internet aims to create infrastructure where privacy is fundamental, participation is voluntary, and the community shares in the value it creates.

## Core Principles

| Principle | Our Commitment |
|---|---|
| **Privacy First** | Protect personal information through encryption and minimal data collection. |
| **Decentralization** | Reduce dependence on centralized infrastructure. |
| **Affordability** | Make participation and access financially accessible. |
| **Transparency** | Keep foundational technology open and auditable. |
| **Community Ownership of Value** | Reward meaningful contributions to the network. |
| **User Control** | Let individuals decide how their devices and resources participate. |

## How It Works

Decent-Internet envisions a distributed network powered partly by voluntary contributions from its participants.

Participants may eventually be able to share:

- **Bandwidth** — Contribute available network capacity.
- **Computing Power** — Allocate unused processing resources.
- **Storage** — Provide available storage for approved network functions.

Resource contributions would be voluntary, measurable, and subject to user-defined limits.

The network's architecture and economic model are still being designed.

## Open Development

We believe infrastructure this important should be open to inspection and independent verification.

Our intention is to develop core network components publicly, including security protocols, network routing, resource accounting, and contribution mechanisms.

Community contributions will be welcomed through a structured review and security process.

## Project Status

**Current Phase: Foundation & Research**

Decent-Internet is in early development. We are establishing its technical architecture, governance principles, privacy commitments, and financial framework.

A working network has not yet been released.
